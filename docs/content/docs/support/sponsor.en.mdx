# Advertising sponsorship

If you want sponsorship placement in project docs, the README, or related pages, email the maintainers to discuss position, duration, and format.

## Suitable sponsorship content

- AI tools, model services, API services, or developer tools.
- Products related to image, video, or text generation workflows.
- Services useful to open-source project users and AI creators.

## Information to include

- Brand or product name.
- Desired placement and duration.
- Links, copy, and creative assets.
- Budget range and collaboration model.
