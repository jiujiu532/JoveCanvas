# Contributor agreement

See the Contributor License Agreement: [CLA.md](https://github.com/jiujiu532/JoveCanvas/blob/main/CLA.md).

Submitting a Pull Request, patch, documentation, asset, or other contribution to this repository means you agree to that agreement. Maintainers may also ask you to reply explicitly in the PR:

```text
I have read and agree to CLA.md.
```

## Before you contribute

- You have the right to submit the related code, documentation, or assets.
- Third-party material is clearly attributed and licensed in a way compatible with this project.
- Your contribution may be released with the project under AGPL-3.0 and any project-related commercial terms.
- Do not disclose security vulnerability details via public Issues—follow the [vulnerability reporting](/en/docs/support/security) process.
